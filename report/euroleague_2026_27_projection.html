What the rosters changed

Each club's pre-season strength is a regression of last season's carried net rating, the prior-season production of the registered roster (PIR per 40 and net RAPM of the top nine, unknown arrivals at replacement level) and continuity, the share of last season's minutes still on the roster. Values in net rating points per 100 possessions.

RAPM: pulled, matched, and mostly redundant

Hackastat's regularised adjusted plus-minus was collected for every Euroleague and EuroCup player-season since 2020-21 (plus ACB, LBA and BCL for the last two seasons) and matched to league player codes for 98% of Euroleague rows. Tested as a roster valuation next to PIR, it did not improve the season-strength forecast.

How much to trust it

Rolling-origin validation: the model is trained on all seasons before the test season and scored on that season's games. Log loss, lower is better; 0.693 is a coin flip.

Read the numbers this way. The classifier beats the home-court base rate clearly and matches a single-feature Elo model; the extra features buy calibration and roster awareness rather than raw accuracy. With under two thousand games that is the realistic ceiling.

  • Strength is static across a simulated season and sampled once per iteration with a 4.5-point spread (the season-prior residual), wider for Beşiktaş's transfer-based prior.
  • Beşiktaş's history is EuroCup, transformed to Euroleague scale with ratios from eight previous promotions; promoted clubs have historically landed near net zero regardless of EuroCup dominance.
  • NBA and domestic-only arrivals carry no production history and count at replacement level, so clubs that recruited from there (ASVEL, Valencia's Gueye) are priced conservatively.
  • Tiebreaks use head-to-head record among tied clubs, then random; point differential is not simulated.
